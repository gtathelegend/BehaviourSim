  2. Figure 4 trains on N observations and evaluates on ALL remaining subsequent observations (9,770 - N).
  This is expected because they represent different evaluation validation subsets.

CLASSIFICATION OF LEARNING CURVE FLATNESS:
  Selected Classification: A. LEGITIMATE FLAT LEARNING CURVE

Detailed Rationale:
  - The training datasets genuinely grow with N (N=50 has 50 rows, N=500 has 500 rows).
  - The models are fresh, distinct XGBoost instances with distinct tree structures and feature importances.
  - The validation sets shrink appropriately from 9,720 to 9,270 rows.
  - The dominant predictive signals (e.g. window_error_rate, streak_incorrect) are strong and easily learned by XGBoost depth=3 within 50 observations.
  - Because the underlying cognitive load dynamics produce clear behavioral markers during overload states, a sample size of N=50 is already sufficient for XGBoost to achieve ~0.962 validation AUC.
